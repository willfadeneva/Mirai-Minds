# Mirai Minds Content Expansion 2

## Added

### Grade 1 alphabet flipcards
- A–Z interactive flipcards.
- Each card includes letter, word, Japanese meaning, picture clue, sound clue, and practice mission.
- Designed for children with zero English knowledge.

### Vocabulary picture flipcards for every grade
- 12 cards per grade.
- Difficulty tags: easy, medium, challenge.
- Japanese meanings included.
- Emoji picture placeholders included so everything remains free and lightweight.
- Can later be replaced with open-source SVG illustrations or a Mirai Minds mascot image system.

### Reading stories
- 20 stories per grade.
- 240 stories total across Grade 1–12.
- Each story includes:
  - title
  - reading type
  - level
  - theme
  - story text
  - focus words
  - comprehension questions
  - answer keys
  - activity suggestion

## Grade-wise additions summary

- Grade 1: A–Z alphabet cards, first-word picture cards, 20 Pre-A1 tiny stories.
- Grade 2: family/weather/food/toy picture cards, 20 Pre-A1 starter stories.
- Grade 3: school/time/town vocabulary, 20 A1 place and routine stories.
- Grade 4: nature/hobbies/past-event vocabulary, 20 A1 story-builder readings.
- Grade 5: adventure/community/helper vocabulary, 20 A2 sequence stories.
- Grade 6: culture/opinion/future-plan vocabulary, 20 A2 presentation stories.
- Grade 7: environment/technology/paragraph vocabulary, 20 A2+ paragraph readings.
- Grade 8: media/history/summary vocabulary, 20 B1 presentation and podcast stories.
- Grade 9: research/data/evidence vocabulary, 20 B1 academic explorer stories.
- Grade 10: essay/viewpoint/global issue vocabulary, 20 B1+ critical reader stories.
- Grade 11: debate/rhetoric/policy vocabulary, 20 B2 debate-builder stories.
- Grade 12: portfolio/professional/academic vocabulary, 20 B2 future communicator stories.
